How to Play AquaGuardian

Protecting our global waterways through advanced autonomous eco-technology.

1. Collect & Recycle Debris
Drive over floating bottles, bags, and nets. Return to a Recycling Buoy (yellow beacon) to offload cargo for Eco-Credits, XP, and WQI points.
2. Hydro-Drift for Mini-Turbos
Turn sharply while at high speed to hydro-drift with water sparks! Hold the drift for over 1.2 seconds to release a powerful Mini-Turbo Boost.
3. Mystery Supply Drops & Power-ups
Look for floating golden Supply Crates! Smash them to trigger confetti jackpots, granting Overcharge Turbo, Giant Magnet Vortexes, and Invincibility Shields.
4. Neutralize Toxic Barrels & Install Bio-Filters
Hold [E] near leaking barrels to tractor-neutralize them. Approach storm sewer pipes and time the bio-filter clamp inside the green zone!
5. Mandatory Learning Quizzes
To advance across the campaign, you must master the SDG 6 educational quiz at the end of each level!
